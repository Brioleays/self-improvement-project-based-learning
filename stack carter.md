2. **Complementary over alternative.** Skip technologies that just duplicate another.
3. **Tools earn their place.** Must teach a new concept, unlock capability, be established, or solve a real problem.

# 🛟 Recovery Protocol

- **Missed a day:** Do the smallest unit of work today. 20 minutes.
- **Missed a week:** Open last project, read last commit, resume next action.
- **Missed a month:** Pick one feature. Ship it this week. Momentum first.
- **Lost motivation:** One kata. Smallest possible.

**Rule:** Return to the smallest next action. Not the plan. Not the vision.
